01 / ORDERING

How to order from us.

Have a look at our coffee, cashews and bundle. When you've decided what you'd like, send us a message on Facebook or Instagram.

We'll let you know what's available, confirm the total and arrange payment with you. Our website doesn't take card payments.

For your security, please don't send payment card details through social media messages.

03 / INGREDIENTS

What goes into our products?

Roasted salted cashews: cashew nuts (98%), iodised salt (1%) and flavour enhancer (MSG 621) (1%). Contains cashews (tree nuts).

Coffee blend: Robusta, Culi (peaberry) and Moka, as listed on the manufacturer's packaging.

Both products are sourced from Vietnam. Check your individual pack for applicable date marking, storage directions and net weight.

The cashew manufacturer states no preservatives; the cashews do contain MSG, a flavour enhancer.

04 / CUSTOMER CARE

Something not right with your order?

If something arrives damaged, incorrect or not as described, please message us with your order details. A photo can help us understand the issue.

We'll discuss an appropriate remedy in accordance with Australian Consumer Law. Your consumer guarantees are not excluded by this policy.

We're a small local business, and we want every order to be a good experience.

05 / YOUR PRIVACY

What happens to your details?

We use the name, contact information, product selection and pickup or delivery details you share to answer enquiries and arrange orders.

Messages sent through Facebook or Instagram are also subject to those platforms' privacy practices. Our website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and interactions.

Please don't share sensitive personal or payment card details in chat. Contact us through our social accounts if you have questions about the information you've provided.
