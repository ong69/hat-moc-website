Good to know,
before you order.

A simple guide to shopping with Hạt Mộc. We want you to feel confident about what you are ordering and how it gets to you.

01 · Ordering & payment

Choose your coffee, cashews or bundle on our website and send us a message through Facebook Messenger or Instagram. We'll confirm your selection, price, availability and how payment will be arranged before you commit to an order.

Our website does not currently process card payments at checkout. Please do not send payment card details through social media messages.

02 · Pickup & local delivery

Pickup: Free by arrangement in Bankstown, NSW. Message us to confirm a suitable collection time and location.

Local delivery: Available by arrangement in surrounding areas. Tell us your suburb and we'll confirm whether delivery is available and any applicable fee before you order.

03 · Product information

Our product collection includes ingredient and allergen details where confirmed from the original manufacturer packaging. Our roasted salted cashews contain cashews (98%), iodised salt (1%) and flavour enhancer (MSG 621) (1%). Allergen: cashews (tree nuts).

Check the physical pack for its applicable date marking, storage directions and net weight. Contact us if you need clarification before purchasing.

04 · Problems with an order

If an item is damaged, incorrect or not as described, message us as soon as possible with your order details and, if helpful, a photo. We'll discuss an appropriate remedy in accordance with Australian Consumer Law. Your consumer guarantees are not excluded by this policy.

05 · Privacy & enquiries

We use the name, contact details, product selection and pickup or delivery information you provide to respond to enquiries and arrange orders. Messages sent via Facebook or Instagram are also subject to those platforms' privacy practices.

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand site visits and interactions. If you have questions about information you've shared with us, contact us through our social accounts. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or payment card information in messages.
