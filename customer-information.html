01 / ORDERING

How to order from us.

Choose your coffee, cashews or bundle on our website. Add your products to the cart, select pickup or delivery, and review the order total before submitting.

We'll email your order summary and payment instructions. Pay using the PayID or bank transfer details in your email, include your order reference, then reply with payment confirmation. We verify payment before preparing your order. No payment is taken on the website.

If an order needs a manual postage quote, wait for the confirmed amount before paying. Never send payment card details by email or message.

02 / PICKUP & DELIVERY

Picking up or getting it delivered?

Free pickup in Bankstown, NSW. Choose pickup at checkout. We'll arrange a suitable collection time and location after confirming your order and payment.

Free local delivery to eligible suburbs. Enter your suburb at checkout to check whether free delivery is available. Other locations can use Australia-wide postage.

Australia-wide postage. Enter your destination to see the available postage estimate. If special packing or multiple parcels require a manual quote, we'll confirm the final amount before payment. After payment clears, we aim to dispatch within two business days; postal transit time is additional.

03 / INGREDIENTS

What goes into our products?

Roasted salted cashews: cashew nuts (98%), iodised salt (1%) and flavour enhancer (MSG 621) (1%). Contains cashews (tree nuts).

Coffee blend: Robusta, Culi (peaberry) and Moka, as listed on the manufacturer's packaging.

Both products are sourced from Vietnam. Check your individual pack for applicable date marking, storage directions and net weight.

The cashew manufacturer states no preservatives; the cashews do contain MSG, a flavour enhancer.

05 / YOUR PRIVACY

What happens to your details?

We use the name, email address, phone number, product selection and pickup or delivery details you provide at checkout to process your order, send confirmation emails and arrange fulfilment.

Order information is sent through our website and email services. Messages sent through Facebook or Instagram are also subject to those platforms' privacy practices. Our website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and interactions.

Please don't share sensitive personal or payment card details in chat. Contact us through our social accounts if you have questions about the information you've provided.
