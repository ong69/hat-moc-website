Five ways to make Vietnamese coffee at home

Start with one short brew and make cà phê sữa đá, egg coffee, coconut coffee, salt coffee or bạc xỉu.

In this guide

  • Start with a concentrated brew
  • Cà phê sữa đá · Vietnamese iced coffee
  • Cà phê trứng · Hanoi egg coffee
  • Cà phê cốt dừa · Coconut coffee
  • Cà phê muối · Salt coffee
  • Bạc xỉu · Milk with a splash of coffee

Start with a concentrated brew

Put 18 g of medium-fine ground coffee in a phin and level it. Set the press on the grounds without compacting them. Add about 20 mL hot water to wet the coffee, wait 30 seconds, then add about 70 mL more. Let the coffee drip through. With an espresso machine, use a double shot as an easy substitute.

Cà phê sữa đá · Vietnamese iced coffee

Brew over 1–2 tablespoons of sweetened condensed milk. Stir until smooth and pour into a glass packed with ice. For iced black coffee, leave the condensed milk out and sweeten to taste.

Cà phê trứng · Hanoi egg coffee

Whisk 2 pasteurised egg yolks with 2 tablespoons of condensed milk until pale and thick, then spoon the foam onto hot coffee. This makes enough topping for two small cups. Serve immediately.

Cà phê cốt dừa · Coconut coffee

Blend 60 mL coconut milk, 1 tablespoon condensed milk and a handful of ice until slushy. Pour the coffee into a chilled glass, spoon the coconut mixture over it and serve straight away.

Cà phê muối · Salt coffee

Whisk 40 mL thickened cream with 1 teaspoon of condensed milk and a small pinch of salt until it thickens but still pours. Pour the cream over hot or iced coffee. Taste before adding any extra salt.

Bạc xỉu · Milk with a splash of coffee

Stir 1 tablespoon condensed milk into 120 mL cold milk. Add ice and top with 20–30 mL of your concentrated coffee. Use the rest for another glass or add it gradually if you prefer a stronger drink.
