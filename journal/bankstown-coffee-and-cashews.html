Vietnamese coffee and roasted cashews in Bankstown

Order Hạt Mộc’s Vietnamese coffee beans and roasted cashews online, then arrange free Bankstown pickup.

In this guide

  • How Bankstown pickup works
  • Check local delivery for your address
  • Pick a bag for how you’ll use it
  • A simple coffee and cashew pairing

How Bankstown pickup works

Choose your items online and select pickup in the order form. Collection is arranged with Hạt Mộc. Follow the confirmation details for the pickup time and location before travelling. If you’re collecting for someone else, have their order reference handy.

Check local delivery for your address

Nearby suburbs such as Yagoona, Greenacre, Condell Park and Chullora appear in our delivery area. Use the postcode and suburb selector at checkout to see the available option for your order. Postage is available if you’re outside the local area.

Pick a bag for how you’ll use it

Our coffee comes in a 500 g pack of whole beans, so you’ll need a grinder if you’re brewing at home. Try the smaller cashew pack if you’re buying for yourself. A larger pack makes sense if you’re putting out a shared bowl, cooking with the nuts or ordering for several people.

A simple coffee and cashew pairing

Try the salted cashews alongside an iced black phin coffee. The salt and toasted flavour make a good match for a coffee with a little bitterness. If you prefer condensed milk, stir it into the coffee before pouring over ice so you can taste the balance as you go.
