How to brew Vietnamese coffee with a phin

A simple step-by-step method for a rich cup without an espresso machine.

In this guide
  • What you need
  • Add the coffee
  • Bloom and brew
  • Taste and adjust

What you need

Use a phin filter, a heatproof cup, Vietnamese ground coffee and hot water. Condensed milk and ice are optional.

Add the coffee

Put around 20 grams of medium-coarse coffee in the filter chamber. Level the grounds gently and fit the press without packing them too tightly.

Bloom and brew

Add a small splash of hot water and wait about 30 seconds. Fill the phin with more water, put on the lid and allow it to drip into the cup. Aim for a steady drip rather than a rush.

Taste and adjust

If it drips too fast, try a slightly finer grind. If it stalls, go coarser or ease the press. Stir in condensed milk or serve over ice if you prefer.
