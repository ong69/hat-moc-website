Buy Vietnamese cashew nuts online in Australia

What to check before ordering roasted Vietnamese cashews online, from price per 100 g to how you’ll store them.

In this guide

  • Compare like with like
  • Choose roasted or raw for your recipe
  • Check date marking and storage
  • Order from Hạt Mộc

Compare like with like

Divide the pack price by its weight in grams and multiply by 100 to find the price per 100 g. Compare salted with salted and whole kernels with whole kernels if appearance matters to you. For shipped orders, include postage before comparing the total.

Choose roasted or raw for your recipe

Roasted cashews are ready for snack bowls and make a good finishing ingredient for noodles. For a blended cashew sauce, raw unsalted nuts are usually the easier choice because you can control the seasoning and keep the flavour mild.

Check date marking and storage

Use the label’s storage instructions. Once opened, keep air and moisture out by closing the bag well or using a clean airtight container. Buy a size that fits the way you snack, especially if you like the crisp texture of freshly opened roasted nuts.
