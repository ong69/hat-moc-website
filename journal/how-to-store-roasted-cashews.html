Cashew guides

How to keep roasted cashews fresh and crunchy

Simple storage tips to protect the texture and flavour of roasted cashews.

In this guide
  • Keep air and moisture out
  • Choose a cool, dark spot
  • Avoid strong odours
  • Check before serving

Keep air and moisture out

After opening, close the original bag tightly or move the nuts into an airtight container. Moisture is one of the main reasons roasted cashews lose their crunch.

Choose a cool, dark spot

Store them away from direct sunlight, the oven and other sources of heat. Follow the product's best-before date and any storage directions on its packaging.

Avoid strong odours

Nuts can pick up smells from nearby foods. A sealed container helps preserve their natural roasted flavour.

Check before serving

If cashews develop an unusual smell, taste or visible mould, discard them. Serve a small bowl at a time and keep the rest sealed.
