Where to buy Vietnamese coffee beans in Sydney

Find whole Vietnamese coffee beans for your phin, with Bankstown pickup or postage across Sydney.

In this guide

  • Choose beans for the cup you want
  • Buying in Western Sydney
  • What Hạt Mộc sells
  • Pick up or order online

Choose beans for the cup you want

For a condensed milk iced coffee, look for a blend with enough body to stand up to both milk and melting ice. A Robusta-led blend is a good starting point. If you usually drink coffee black, try your first brew with a little extra hot water so you can judge the roast without the concentration of a short phin pour.

Buying in Western Sydney

If you shop around Bankstown, Cabramatta or Canley Vale, compare the bags as closely as you compare the cafés. Look for whole beans if you have a grinder, and check the label for the blend and date marking. If you buy pre-ground coffee elsewhere, ask what brewer the grind is intended for. Some packaged coffee is much finer than a phin needs.

What Hạt Mộc sells

Our Hanoi Coffee Beans come as a 500 g whole-bean blend. The pack lists Robusta, Culi (peaberry) and Moka. For a first phin brew, start with 18 g of medium-fine grounds and about 90 mL of hot water. Adjust the grind on the next cup if it runs through too quickly or stalls.
