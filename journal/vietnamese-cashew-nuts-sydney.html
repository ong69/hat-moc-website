Where to buy Vietnamese roasted cashews in Sydney

Compare pack sizes, check ingredients and choose Bankstown pickup, local delivery or Sydney postage.

Decide what you’re buying them for

Cashews for snacking and cashews for cooking have different jobs. A whole, dry-roasted or salt-roasted nut is easy to serve straight from the bag. Broken pieces are perfectly useful in fried rice, noodle salads or homemade granola, where the shape barely matters.

Choose a pack size

Hạt Mộc offers roasted salted cashews in 150 g, 300 g and 500 g packs. Go small if you’re tasting them for the first time. If you’re buying a shared snack or stocking the pantry, pick the size you’ll use while the nuts still have the crunch you like.

Read the ingredient list

Our pack declares cashews and iodised salt. Cashew is a tree nut allergen. Check the label for the full declaration and any other advice before serving to guests. Taste one before adding them to a recipe and adjust the salt in the rest of the dish.

Order from Sydney

Choose arranged Bankstown pickup or check your suburb for local delivery. For orders further across Sydney, enter your destination in the order form for postage. The store shows pack sizes and current prices alongside the coffee collection.
