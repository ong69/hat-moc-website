Buy Vietnamese coffee beans online in Australia

A practical guide to choosing a Vietnamese whole-bean blend and ordering it from anywhere in Australia.

In this guide

  • Check the blend and format first
  • Start with the way you brew
  • Make a 500 g bag work for you
  • Ordering outside Sydney

Check the blend and format first

A product listing should tell you whether the bag contains whole beans or ground coffee. Whole beans give you room to adjust for a phin, espresso machine or French press. If the label says Culi, it refers to peaberry, the rounded single seed that sometimes forms inside a coffee cherry.

Start with the way you brew

For a phin, use a medium-fine grind and a short pour to keep the coffee strong enough for ice. For a French press, start coarser and use a larger volume of water. If you usually drink espresso with milk, try a small cup first and adjust the dose and extraction to your taste.

Make a 500 g bag work for you

At 18 g per phin, 500 g makes around 27 brews. Keep the bag tightly closed in a cool, dry cupboard and grind only what you’re about to brew. Marking the opening date on the pack helps you judge how quickly you get through it.

Ordering outside Sydney

Hạt Mộc ships from Sydney across Australia, including Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide, Perth and Canberra. Enter your delivery suburb and postcode in the store to check postage for your cart. The same order form covers coffee and cashews, so you can check the total for both before placing an order.
