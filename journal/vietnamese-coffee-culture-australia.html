Where Vietnamese coffee culture thrives in Australia

Explore the food neighbourhoods where Vietnamese coffee feels at home, from Western Sydney to Melbourne and Brisbane.

In this guide

  • Western Sydney · Bankstown and Cabramatta
  • Melbourne · Footscray and Richmond
  • Brisbane · Inala
  • Bring a favourite cup home

Western Sydney · Bankstown and Cabramatta

In Bankstown and Cabramatta, make coffee part of a food outing. Order a bánh mì or sit down for a bowl of phở, then look for cà phê sữa đá on the menu. Canley Vale is another nearby stop if you’re taking the train through the south-west.

Melbourne · Footscray and Richmond

Footscray’s food precinct and Victoria Street in Richmond are good places to explore Vietnamese menus. Look for iced coffee alongside lunch, or ask whether the café brews with a phin if you want to see the slower table-side method.

Brisbane · Inala

Inala is home to a Vietnamese food precinct where you can make a morning of the bakeries, grocers and cafés. If you prefer a less sweet cup, order cà phê đen đá, iced black coffee, and ask about sugar before ordering.

Bring a favourite cup home

Take note of whether you liked the coffee’s roast, sweetness or texture. Start with the iced condensed milk version at home, then adjust the milk or try a glass of bạc xỉu. A small phin and a heatproof glass are enough to get started.
