Shopping guides

How to choose Vietnamese coffee beans for home

A practical guide to roast, grind and flavour before buying your next bag.

In this guide
  • Start with how you brew
  • Robusta or a blend?
  • Check freshness and storage
  • Find your preferred cup

Start with how you brew

A traditional phin is forgiving, but the grind still matters. Choose coffee that suits your brewer and ask whether the beans are whole or pre-ground.

Robusta or a blend?

Vietnamese Robusta is known for its strong body and distinctive bitterness. Blends can soften the flavour, while roast level changes the balance of sweetness and intensity.

Check freshness and storage

Look for a clear roast or packing date where available. Keep beans sealed away from heat, sunlight and moisture, and grind shortly before brewing for the fullest aroma.

Find your preferred cup

Try a small amount first, then adjust the dose, grind and water. If you enjoy Vietnamese iced coffee with condensed milk, a full-bodied roast is a good place to begin.
