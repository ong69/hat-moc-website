Coffee culture

The Vietnamese phin coffee ritual, explained

Why a small metal filter and a few quiet minutes are part of the experience.

A slower way to make coffee

A phin is a compact metal filter that sits over a cup or glass. Hot water passes slowly through ground coffee, making a concentrated brew without a large machine.

What makes the ritual special

The waiting is part of the appeal. At home or in a café, a few minutes of dripping coffee can turn an ordinary break into a small pause in the day.

How people enjoy it

Some drink their coffee black, while others add condensed milk or pour it over ice. There is no single correct cup: sweetness and strength are personal.

Bring it home

Start with fresh coffee, a clean phin and hot water just off the boil. Adjust your grind and dose until the drip and flavour suit you.
