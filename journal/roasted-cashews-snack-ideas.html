Cashew guides

Five easy ways to enjoy roasted cashews

From afternoon snacks to salads, here are simple ways to enjoy their crunch.

In this guide
  • Enjoy them as they are
  • Add crunch to salads
  • Finish a stir-fry
  • Make a snack mix
  • Pair with Vietnamese coffee

Enjoy them as they are

A small bowl of roasted cashews makes an easy snack alongside tea or coffee. Their rich flavour needs little extra.

Add crunch to salads

Sprinkle chopped cashews over a crunchy cabbage or herb salad just before serving to keep their texture.

Finish a stir-fry

Toss cashews through cooked vegetables at the end rather than simmering them in sauce.

Make a snack mix

Combine cashews with other nuts or dried fruit in a sealed container for an easy portion to take out.

Pair with Vietnamese coffee

Serve a handful with strong Vietnamese coffee for a simple contrast of nutty crunch and bold flavour.
