THE POUR. THE AROMA. THE CRUNCH.
OUR ROOTS · HANOI, VIETNAM

Bold Vietnamese Coffee.
Perfectly Roasted Cashews.

In Vietnam, iced coffee is more than a way to cool down. It’s part of everyday life — a slow morning at a street-side café, a conversation with friends, or a welcome break from the heat. Strong coffee poured over ice, made to be enjoyed rather than rushed.

That’s the taste we wanted to bring to Hạt Mộc. Our robusta coffee is carefully sourced from Hanoi for its deep aroma and bold, full-bodied flavour. Freshly prepared, with no unnecessary additives — just the rich, unmistakable taste of Vietnamese coffee.

And the cashews? We choose them for the same reason: quality you can taste. Golden roasted, satisfyingly crunchy and delicious on their own or alongside a cold glass of coffee.

Simple things, done well. A little piece of Hanoi, wherever you enjoy it.
